- Fixed screen shares freezing for viewers: frames can no longer burst far past your upload (the encoder's buffer is an eighth of a second instead of a full second, and packets are paced to your bitrate), keyframes come at most once a second, and when the network falls behind a frame is skipped instead of breaking the video.
- The bitrate no longer runs into the same packet loss every few seconds: it remembers where loss started and creeps up slowly near it.
- Hardware encoding for every GPU on Windows: Intel GPUs (Arc and the graphics built into Intel processors) and any other GPU now encode streams on the GPU through Windows Media Foundation, in H264, H265 and AV1 where the GPU supports them. NVIDIA and AMD keep their own encoders, and the processor encodes only when no GPU can.
