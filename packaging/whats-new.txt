- New: Game Mode (Settings > Game activity). While a game runs, only calls, mentions and DMs make a sound or notification, animations pause, and memory goes back to your game.
- New: desktop notifications on Windows. Click one to open the conversation.
- Smoother animations for menus, popups and buttons, and Reduce motion in Appearance to turn them off.
- Screen sharing uses half the memory on NVIDIA cards, and holds 1080p at 144 and 240 fps.
