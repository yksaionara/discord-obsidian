- Fixed streams (and calls) dropping with "the system lacked sufficient buffer space" (error 10055): a full network buffer now drops one packet instead of ending the connection.
- Streams start at about 4 Mbit/s, like Discord, and climb to your quality's cap while your connection keeps up, instead of starting at the cap and flooding it.
- Packets resent to viewers are capped, so heavy loss can't double the traffic, and the network buffers are bigger.
- Updates: the green Update pill shows up whenever an update is ready, in a call or not. Click it to update now; otherwise it installs the next time you open the app. The app no longer restarts on its own to update, and it checks every 30 minutes instead of every 6 hours.
