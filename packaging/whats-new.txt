- Screen sharing is much sharper and smoother: streams start at the full bitrate for the chosen quality and only lower it when packets are actually lost (they used to get stuck around 1.5 Mbit/s).
- No more choppy frames on Windows: packet pacing no longer stalls on Windows' 15.6 ms timer, and keyframes no longer cause stutter cascades.
- Sharing a game window uses full-speed capture (up to 360 fps) whenever the game is in front, even if it was alt-tabbed when you started. Alt-tabbing out of a borderless game no longer shows viewers your desktop.
- The stream no longer restarts its encoder when the window's size wobbles by a pixel or two.
- The voice panel and your account panel are now one connected card, like Discord's.
- New add-on, Game Activity Toggle (Settings > Add-ons, on by default): a controller button beside mute and deafen hides or shows your game in one click.
- Undeafening now turns your microphone back on if it was on before you deafened.
